Privacy Policy for Funny Dad Jokes

Last updated: [July 13, 2026]

Funny Dad Jokes ("the App") is an automated tool that posts a daily joke to a Facebook and/or Instagram Page on a scheduled basis using the Meta Graph API.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal data from end users who view the Page's posts.

The only credential the App uses is a Page Access Token, generated and provided by the app owner, which is used solely to authenticate scheduled posts to the connected Page.

2. Data Storage

No user data is stored by the App. Joke content used for posting is either hard-coded, pulled from a static list, or generated via artificial intelligence, and is not linked to any individual user.

3. Data Sharing

The App does not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13, as it does not collect user data at all.

5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
